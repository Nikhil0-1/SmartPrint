# GITAM SMARTPRINT - OPERATOR & CAMPUS STAFF GUIDE

## 1. Daily Startup & Routine Checks
1. Ensure the desktop monitor is powered ON and displaying the **GITAM SMARTPRINT** dynamic QR code screen.
2. Verify all 3 indicator badges at the bottom of the screen:
   - **HP USB PRINTER**: 🟢 READY
   - **INTERNET & BACKEND**: 🟢 CONNECTED
   - **KIOSK HARDWARE**: 🟢 AVAILABLE
3. Check that the paper tray is filled with standard A4 75/80 GSM paper.
4. Verify toner/cartridge levels on the HP LaserJet printer.

---

## 2. Handling Paper Jams or Physical Out-of-Paper
1. Open the Admin Console at `/admin` and toggle **Maintenance Mode** on the affected kiosk.
2. Clear the physical paper jam or refill the paper tray.
3. Close the printer cover and wait for the HP printer status light to turn solid green.
4. On the Admin Console, click **Print Test Page** to confirm operational status.
5. Disable Maintenance Mode. The kiosk will generate a fresh QR code and resume student sessions.
